## Now

I build products at [@SkyliveLLC](https://github.com/SkyliveLLC). Most of that work lives in private repositories, so it barely shows up in my public activity. This is what I have shipped there:

| Project | What it is | What I worked on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skylive | Streaming platform with a social community (Laravel Octane + Reverb API, Nuxt client) | Community module end to end: posts with multi-image uploads to R2, threaded replies, likes with optimistic updates, reposts, reports and a moderation dashboard. Roles and permissions, i18n across the client. |
| Ascen | Appointment booking SaaS for businesses (Laravel 13, Filament 5, Livewire, Reverb, Expo app) | Booking wizard, first-run onboarding for owners, promotions and deposits, client reviews, WhatsApp template approval, subscription plans and the public business page redesign. |
| Ascentist | Multi-tenant dental clinic platform (Laravel 13, Inertia, Vue 3) | Drag and drop site builder for each clinic, appointment deposits with Wompi, six-month recalls, family booking, no-show reports and the clinic panel. |
| Restaurant BKN | QR table ordering for restaurants, multi-tenant (Laravel, Inertia, Vue, Filament) | Guest menu and ordering flow, per-restaurant theming and typefaces, full interface translation, platform roles. |
| Nexora | Internal CRM to sell the company's own products (Laravel, Inertia, Vue) | Built from the first commit: contacts, Kanban opportunities with stages per product, activities and follow-ups, metrics dashboard, roles and policies. |
| [Lienzo](https://github.com/SkyliveLLC/lienzo) | Open source landing page builder you add to your own app | Editor work for v0.2.0: canvas zoom and pan, context menu, multi-selection edits, pinned elements and navbar templates. |

Before that I worked at Divergente / CodePyme, Aguas de Sincé and Teleperformance Colombia, where I also led a small Scrum team.
